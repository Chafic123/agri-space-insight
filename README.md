# Welcome to your project

The project is an interactive farm simulater that leverages real NASA satellite imagery and climate data. Users take on the role of a farm manager, making decisions about crop planting, irrigation, fertilization, and livestock management, while observing the immediate effects of their choices on crop growth, resource use, and environmental sustainability.
